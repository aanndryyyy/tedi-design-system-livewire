height="auto" starts at the content height, within the default 8–92% workspace limits. Drag the top edge to resize. Edit the form, drag near the maximum, then make the workspace smaller: the height is clamped and your edits remain. Restore the workspace size: the panel keeps its height, and you can drag it taller again. Minimize, then click the title or bar to restore the saved height. The handle is hidden while minimized. To try pixel limits, close the panel, set minHeight=160 and maxHeight=400 in Controls, then reopen.
