`stickyLastColumn` mirrors `stickyFirstColumn` on the trailing edge — it freezes the last column (here a **Muuda** actions column) against the right edge while the body scrolls horizontally underneath. The divider is drawn on the frozen block's left edge. Any trailing control columns (e.g. an expand toggle placed after the `'content'` sentinel via `controlColumnOrder`) freeze together with the last content column.
